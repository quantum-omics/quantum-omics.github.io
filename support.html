Thank you for wanting to support us

We are getting our 501(c)(3) status in place before opening donations — here is where things stand, and how you can help today.

Where we are

Quantum Omics Foundation is a non-profit organisation, and we are in the process of obtaining 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status. We are holding off on donations until that comes through, so that when you give, the tax position is certain.

Once that status is granted we will say so on this page, list our EIN, and open a proper donation route.

Ways you can help right now

Support is not only financial, and the following are open today:

  • Use the software. Everything we build is open source and free. Using it, filing issues, and telling us what breaks is genuinely useful. Our code is on GitHub.
  • Collaborate on the research. If you run a lab or hold data that could test our models, we would like to hear from you.
  • Teach with our materials. Our educational content is free to use in courses and workshops.
  • Tell someone. We are small and largely unknown. A mention to a colleague who works at this intersection helps more than it sounds like it should.
  • Register your interest in giving. Write to us and we will let you know once donations open, rather than you having to check back.
