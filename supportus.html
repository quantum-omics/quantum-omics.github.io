Other Ways to Support

  • Sponsor a Researcher: Directly fund the work of a brilliant mind pushing the boundaries of quantum biology.
  • Expand CADH Cell-Type Coverage: Support the computational resources needed to add new driving Hamiltonians for under-represented cell types and disease-specific variants in the Cell Atlas of Driver Hamiltonians.
  • Sponsor a Disease Perturbation Study: Help us launch a research initiative that maps the Hamiltonian signature $\delta H$ of a specific disease using QVO and the Q-EHR, identifying diagnostic and therapeutic targets.
  • Institutional Giving Portal: For foundations, corporations, and major donors seeking deeper engagement and tailored impact opportunities. Contact us for more details.
