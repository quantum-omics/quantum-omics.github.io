Get Involved

We are a small foundation and the work is open. Whether you write code, run a lab, teach, or simply want to help fund the research, there is a way in.

Join Our Global Community

Join Our Research & Education Community

Collaborate with us in advancing computational cell modelling.

Our work is powered by a global community of researchers, students, educators, and computational scientists. Your involvement accelerates cutting-edge research, contributes to open-source tools, and helps train the next generation of interdisciplinary scientists. As a non-profit organization, we seek funding to conduct these activities.

Research & Education Opportunities

  • Student Internship – Graduate and undergraduate opportunities to gain experience in computational cell modelling (seeking funding support).
  • Research Collaboration – Collaborate on multi-omics data analysis, algorithm development, and simulations.
  • Education Research – Conduct research on educational methodologies and support curriculum development for bioinformatics and quantum computing.
  • Open-Source Development – Contribute to our GitHub repositories, documentation, and computational tools (flexible).
  • Curriculum Development – Help create educational materials, tutorials, and course content (project-based).

Other Ways to Support

  • Sponsor a researcher: Fund a specific piece of the research programme. Available once donations open.
  • Extend the Cell Atlas: Support the computing needed to cover cell types that are under-represented today.
  • Back a disease study: Help launch a study of how a specific disease shifts a cell away from its healthy state, ident diagnostic and therapeutic targets.
  • Institutional Giving Portal: For foundations, corporations, and major donors seeking deeper engagement and tailored impact opportunities. Contact us for more details.
